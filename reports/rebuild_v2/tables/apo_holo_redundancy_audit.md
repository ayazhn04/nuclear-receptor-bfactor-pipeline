# APO/HOLO Redundancy & Dominance Audit

> Checks whether a receptor's APO or HOLO group is dominated by a single repeated ligand. Publication/deposition-series and mutation metadata are **not tracked** anywhere in this pipeline -- that specific check is reported as not assessable with current metadata rather than fabricated. No PDB deposition is assumed to be a fully independent biological experiment. Dominance threshold: a single ligand accounting for >50% of a group's entries is flagged.
