## Limitations

- 41 receptors and 10 features: limited power for any clustering method.
- Features are professor-defined helices only; inter-helix regions are not modeled as features.
- Imputed cells reflect elements the professor table does not define for that receptor; they are placeholders, not measurements (see the imputation section).
